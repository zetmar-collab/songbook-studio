### Short description  *(limit 500 characters)*

Songbook Studio is a fully offline songbook for musicians and lyricists. Keep your lyrics, chords and song ideas in one place instead of spreading them across Word, a notes app and the cloud. Save lyric versions, record voice memos of your melodies, generate chords with AI, transpose them to your own key, and export a single song or your whole songbook to PDF, DOCX or Markdown.

### Description  *(limit 10,000 characters)*
```
Do you write songs? Chances are your lyrics live in three places at once right now: a few Word files, notes on your phone, and something in the cloud. Songbook Studio brings all of it into one desktop app — and it works without an internet connection.

YOUR SONG CATALOG
Every song has a title, artist, key and tags, and you can star the ones that matter. Full-text search finds a song by any fragment of its lyrics or chords, and it ignores accents, so you will find it even if you type without them.

VERSION HISTORY
Rewrote a verse and now prefer the previous take? Save lyric versions as snapshots and restore any of them with one click. No more "lyrics_v2_final_revised.docx".

VOICE NOTES
Melodies tend to arrive when you have nowhere to put them. Record one straight into the app, attached to the right song. Recordings stay on your computer.

AI CHORDS AND TRANSPOSITION
Add your own API key (OpenRouter or Google Gemini) and the app will write chords over your lyrics. One button transposes them up or down into the key you actually sing in. The app also fetches the list of free OpenRouter models along with their context limits, so you can choose with full information.

EXPORTS THAT ARE ACTUALLY USEFUL
Export a single song to PDF, DOCX or Markdown — or your entire songbook into one file, complete with a cover page and table of contents. Ready for rehearsal, for a gig, or to send to your band.

IMPORT FROM WORD
Already have lyrics in .docx files? Load them in one click, without copying and pasting.

OFFLINE BY DEFAULT, YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no sign-in, and no internet connection by default. Lyrics, recordings and settings are all stored locally. Data only leaves your computer if you enter an API key yourself and use the AI feature.

BACKUPS
The app backs up its database when you close it, keeping the last 10 copies, and lets you point backups at any folder you like — for example one that syncs to your cloud drive. You can also back up and restore manually at any time.

EVERYDAY COMFORT
Light, dark or system theme. Interface in English and Polish. Your changes save automatically as you type.

WHO IT IS FOR
Lyricists, singers, guitarists, bands keeping a shared repertoire, and creators working with Suno AI or other generators who need their lyrics organised.

OPEN SOURCE
Songbook Studio is free and released under the MIT license. The source code is publicly available on GitHub, so you can verify exactly what the app does with your data.
```
